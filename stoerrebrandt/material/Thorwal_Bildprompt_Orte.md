# Thorwal — Bildprompts für Orte

Acht Schauplätze, jeder als einzelnes Bild im Querformat. Anders als bei den Figurenbögen steht hier kein Rahmenwerk: Die Orte sollen wie Szenenillustrationen aus einem Regionalband wirken, nicht wie Porträtplatten.

## Stilblock (identisch in allen acht Prompts)

> Painted scene illustration in the style of an old illustrated roleplaying regional sourcebook, landscape format. Late-medieval northern harbour city built of timber, tarred planks and grey stone. Northern coastal palette of weathered oak, sea grey, moss green, amber lamplight and brass. Soft overcast daylight with low sun breaking through, salt haze, gulls. Lived-in and busy, never grim or ruined. Visible paper grain. No text, no labels, no signage lettering, no modern elements, no people in close-up portrait framing.

---

## O1 — Stoerrebrandt-Kontor (WS02)

[Stilblock] A southern-style merchant counting house standing among northern timber halls: two storeys of pale plastered brick under a steep roof of red clay tiles, unmistakably foreign among tarred wood and turf. Wide loading doors standing open, crates and barrels on a clean swept yard, a crane beam above. Clerks at work, a laden cart, nothing furtive. Prosperous, orderly, welcoming. Afternoon light on the red tiles.

**Hinweis:** Dieses Bild trägt Knoten 3, 5, 9 und 10. Es muss einladend aussehen. Keine Schatten, keine Wachen, keine verschlossenen Tore.

---

## O2 — Hafenmeisterei am Bodirufer (BU02)

[Stilblock] A harbourmaster's office on a broad river quay, low and long, built of heavy timber with a shingled roof and a small watchtower. Outside, a crowded working waterfront: longships and broad-bellied trading vessels moored side by side, rope, nets, stacked timber, men hauling cargo. Inside the open door, shelves of ledgers and a desk by a window. Busy, international, loud.

---

## O3 — Kartothek (WS08)

[Stilblock] The interior of an extraordinary map collection: a tall timber hall lined floor to ceiling with pigeonhole shelves full of rolled charts, wide flat drawers, sea charts pinned to slanted reading desks. Dust in shafts of window light, brass dividers and rulers, a globe of beaten copper. Two scholars at work, one on a ladder. Quiet, scholarly, warm.

---

## O4 — Schule der Hellsicht (WS05)

[Stilblock] A small, austere academy of magic in a northern city, outwardly almost modest: a stone and timber building with narrow tall windows and a single domed observation room above. Inside, workbenches with lenses, mirrors, prisms, brass instruments and sealed glass vessels. Scholarly rather than mystical. No glowing effects, no floating objects, no pentagrams, no robed cultists.

**Hinweis:** Magie wird in Thorwal geduldet, nicht gefeiert. Die Akademie darf kein Machtzentrum sein. Je nüchterner, desto besser.

---

## O5 — Swafnir-Halla (T05)

[Stilblock] The great hall of a northern sea god, built like an upturned ship: a vast timber nave with curved roof beams like a hull's ribs, carved whale and wave motifs on pillars, a long central hearth, benches along the walls, a stone basin of seawater at the far end. Firelight and smoke. Warm, communal, solemn without being gloomy. IMPORTANT: not a Christian church — no cross, no altar with crucifix, no pews, no stained-glass saints, no bell tower.

---

## O6 — Folkehalla (OS04)

[Stilblock] The assembly hall of a free northern city: a wide timber hall with tiered benches in a horseshoe around an open floor, a speaker's stone in the centre, carved beams, banners of ship communities hanging from the rafters. Daylight from high windows. Empty and waiting, or half-filled with arriving captains. Dignified, plain, no thrones.

---

## O7 — Glücklicher Zechpreller (G04)

[Stilblock] A crowded harbour tavern in the foreign quarter of a northern city: low beams, a long trestle table, a fire, lamps of fish oil, sailors from several lands playing dice, drying coats steaming on hooks, a cat on a barrel. Loud, smoky, friendly in a rough way. Warm amber interior light against a grey window.

---

## O8 — Werft Aril Arnesson (BU03)

[Stilblock] A large respected shipyard on a river bank: the half-built hull of a northern longship on the stocks, ribs exposed like a skeleton, scaffolding, stacks of seasoned oak planks, steam rising from a plank-bending pit, coils of rope, the smell of tar made visible as dark smoke. Carpenters at work. Craft and competence, nothing menacing.

---

## Optional: O9 — Das Nordlandlager (nur als alte Seekarte)

[Kein Stilblock.] A detail from an old nautical chart on aged vellum: a northern coastline with scattered islands, hand-drawn in brown ink, soundings and compass rose, a small trading post marked on one island with a simple symbol and an annotation in a different, later hand. Worn folds, water staining, a torn corner. No photographic realism, no modern cartography, no legible modern lettering.

**Hinweis:** Das Lager selbst bekommt in Teil 3 bewusst kein Bild. Es existiert nur als Eintrag auf einer alten Karte, und seine Lage gehört zu Teil 4. Ein fertiges Bild der Insel würde die Enthüllung vorwegnehmen.

---

## Reihenfolge für die Erstellung

Wenn du nicht alle brauchst: O1 und O2 zuerst, die tragen den Einstieg. Dann O5 und O7 für die gesellschaftliche Ebene, O3 und O4 für die Ermittlung, O6 und O8 zuletzt.

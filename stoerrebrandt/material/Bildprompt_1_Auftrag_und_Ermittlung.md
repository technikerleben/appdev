# Bildprompt 1 — Auftrag und Ermittlung

Arvid Stoerrebrandt · Alrik Mennig · Elira Vardis · Ramon di Serrano

## Prompt

Four character portraits arranged in a 2x2 grid on a single aged parchment sheet. Each portrait sits inside its own ornate hand-drawn border of dark ink and faded gold, like plates in an old illustrated rulebook. Beneath each frame a small engraved brass name plaque with the character's name in elegant serif capitals. Painted illustration style, muted palette of parchment cream, slate blue, rust orange and tarnished gold, soft rainy light, visible paper grain and foxing at the edges. Late-medieval northern harbour city, no modern clothing, no text other than the four name plaques.

Top left, plaque "ARVID STOERREBRANDT": a merchant patriarch in his mid fifties, grey-streaked beard, dark expensive wool without ornament, heavy signet ring, quiet watchful expression, the look of a man doing sums while he listens.

Top right, plaque "ALRIK MENNIG": a small stooped clerk, collar one size too wide, nervous ink-stained hands, eyes avoiding the viewer, a ledger held like a shield.

Bottom left, plaque "ELIRA VARDIS": a woman in her late twenties, pale and sleep-deprived, dark hair pulled back severely, neat but slept-in clerk's clothing, clutching a bundle of papers against her chest, ink stains on her index and middle finger.

Bottom right, plaque "RAMON DI SERRANO": a lean southern duellist in his early thirties, short pointed beard, broad-brimmed hat dripping with rain, dark soaked cloak, rapier hilt just visible beneath it, upright fencer's posture.

# Bildprompt 4 — Die Gegenseite

Baldric Voss · Hasko Rellin · Torvin Barg · Wachposten

## Prompt

Four character portraits arranged in a 2x2 grid on a single aged parchment sheet. Each portrait sits inside its own ornate hand-drawn border of dark ink and faded gold, like plates in an old illustrated rulebook. Beneath each frame a small engraved brass name plaque with the character's name in elegant serif capitals. Painted illustration style, muted palette of parchment cream, slate blue, rust orange and tarnished gold, soft rainy light, visible paper grain and foxing at the edges. Late-medieval northern harbour city, no modern clothing, no text other than the four name plaques.

Top left, plaque "BALDRIC VOSS": a heavyset dock overseer in his early fifties, weathered face, short grey queue at the neck, oilskin slung over one shoulder, wax tablet at his belt, stylus behind his ear, annoyed rather than surprised.

Top right, plaque "HASKO RELLIN": a mercenary captain in his late thirties, close-cropped hair, grey weathered face, dark oilcloth coat over a leather jerkin, calm and unhurried, the stillness of a man used to being obeyed.

Bottom left, plaque "TORVIN BARG": a dock thug in a broad worker's jacket, hood pulled low, broken nose, holding a heavy cargo club instead of a blade.

Bottom right, plaque "WACHPOSTEN": a hired watchman with a loaded crossbow, a shuttered lantern at his feet, hunched against the rain, face half in shadow.

## Negativ

no gore, no blood, no visible wounds, no religious symbols, no modern clothing, no firearms, no text besides the four name plaques, no photorealism, no anime style

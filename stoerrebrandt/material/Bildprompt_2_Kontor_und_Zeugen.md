# Bildprompt 2 — Kontor und Zeugen

Tjark Hagedorn · Schwester Minai · Corvin Rehn · Gerold Damm

## Prompt

Four character portraits arranged in a 2x2 grid on a single aged parchment sheet. Each portrait sits inside its own ornate hand-drawn border of dark ink and faded gold, like plates in an old illustrated rulebook. Beneath each frame a small engraved brass name plaque with the character's name in elegant serif capitals. Painted illustration style, muted palette of parchment cream, slate blue, rust orange and tarnished gold, soft rainy light, visible paper grain and foxing at the edges. Late-medieval northern harbour city, no modern clothing, no text other than the four name plaques.

Top left, plaque "TJARK HAGEDORN": a trade office manager in his early fifties, carefully trimmed beard, fresh white collar, immaculate hands, grey and dark blue wool, eyes darting nervously despite the composed face.

Top right, plaque "SCHWESTER MINAI": a hearth priestess in her mid forties, plain undyed grey-blue robe with an apron, sleeves rolled up, hair under a simple cloth. Around her neck, clearly visible on a leather cord, hangs a small carved wooden amulet in the shape of a goose, the sacred bird of her faith. Tired stone-hard face that has learned to stay warm, burnt fingertips from the soup pot. IMPORTANT: her faith is not Christian. She wears no cross, no crucifix, no rosary, no wimple or nun's habit, no church iconography of any kind. The goose amulet is her only religious symbol.

Bottom left, plaque "CORVIN REHN": a former sailor not yet forty but weathered far beyond it, untrimmed beard, open collar, cracked hands with two fingernails missing, faded salt-stained sailor's jacket, haunted sideways look.

Bottom right, plaque "GEROLD DAMM": a smuggler in his mid fifties, grey stubble, narrow eyes, a much-patched coat too good for a poor man, oil-stained fingers, back to the wall.

## Negativ

no cross, no crucifix, no rosary, no nun's habit, no wimple, no church architecture, no halo, no Christian iconography, no modern clothing, no text besides the four name plaques, no photorealism, no anime style

## Hinweis

Falls das Modell trotzdem ein Kreuz einbaut: Amulett einzeln nachgenerieren und einsetzen, oder den Minai-Rahmen allein mit dem Zusatz „close-up of a plain wooden goose pendant on a leather cord, no other jewellery" wiederholen.

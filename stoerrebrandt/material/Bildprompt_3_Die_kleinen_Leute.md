# Bildprompt 3 — Die kleinen Leute

Joran Feldt · Nivesa Holmbach · Fenrik · Rika Borken

## Prompt

Four character portraits arranged in a 2x2 grid on a single aged parchment sheet. Each portrait sits inside its own ornate hand-drawn border of dark ink and faded gold, like plates in an old illustrated rulebook. Beneath each frame a small engraved brass name plaque with the character's name in elegant serif capitals. Painted illustration style, muted palette of parchment cream, slate blue, rust orange and tarnished gold, soft rainy light, visible paper grain and foxing at the edges. Late-medieval northern harbour city, no modern clothing, no text other than the four name plaques.

Top left, plaque "JORAN FELDT": a fifteen-year-old street boy, far too thin for his clothes, wet hair plastered to his forehead, a yellowing bruise under one eye, oversized boots, a blue cloth band on his wrist, a small carved wooden animal held in one hand.

Top right, plaque "NIVESA HOLMBACH": a seventeen-year-old fisherman's daughter, wiry, sunburnt forearms, sandy braids, fisher's clothing with a faint sheen of scales on the sleeves, salt-stiff apron, looking a moment too long at something off-frame.

Bottom left, plaque "FENRIK": a ten-year-old messenger boy, dirty cheeks, a hood slipping down over his nose, barefoot in the rain, already half turned to run.

Bottom right, plaque "RIKA BORKEN": an old seamstress, grey hair under a bonnet, a pincushion strapped to her wrist, arms folded under her apron, standing in a doorway and watching the street.

## Negativ

no religious symbols, no crosses, no modern clothing, no weapons, nothing sexualised, no text besides the four name plaques, no photorealism, no anime style

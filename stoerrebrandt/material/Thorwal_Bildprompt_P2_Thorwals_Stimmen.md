# Thorwal — Bildprompt P2: Thorwals Stimmen

Asgrimm Ragnarsson · Kjaska Brydasdottir · Ôle Swafgardsson · Cellyana von Khunchom

## Prompt

Four character portraits arranged in a 2x2 grid on a single aged parchment sheet. Each portrait sits inside its own ornate hand-drawn border of dark ink and tarnished brass, like plates in an old illustrated rulebook. Beneath each frame a small engraved brass name plaque with the character's name in elegant serif capitals. Painted illustration style, northern coastal palette of weathered oak, sea grey, moss green, amber lamplight and brass, soft overcast daylight, visible paper grain and foxing at the edges. Late-medieval northern harbour city, no modern clothing, no text other than the four name plaques.

Top left, plaque "ASGRIMM RAGNARSSON": the elected leader of a northern seafaring city, a man of weight and plain authority, greying beard neatly kept, good but unshowy wool and leather, a heavy silver clasp at the shoulder. He looks like a man used to hearing two sides of an argument before speaking. No crown, no throne, no visible rank beyond the clasp.

Top right, plaque "KJASKA BRYDASDOTTIR": a ship's captain and political speaker in her forties, weather-tanned, hair braided back out of the wind, practical sea clothing of oiled leather and wool, a sea chart rolled under one arm. Direct, impatient, already half turned towards the door.

Bottom left, plaque "ÔLE SWAFGARDSSON": an elderly priest of the northern sea god, broad and weather-beaten, long grey hair and beard, simple blue-grey robes over seaman's clothing, a carved whale-tooth pendant on a leather cord. Kind and immovable at once. IMPORTANT: his faith is not Christian — no cross, no crucifix, no rosary, no monk's habit or tonsure, no church iconography of any kind. The whale pendant is his only religious symbol.

Bottom right, plaque "CELLYANA VON KHUNCHOM": a scholar of magic in her fifties, southern-born, fine dark robes with restrained embroidery, a brass instrument of lenses and rings in her hands, reading spectacles pushed up. Precise, sceptical, mid-thought. No pointed hat, no glowing effects, no staff.

## Negativ

no cross, no crucifix, no rosary, no monk's habit, no church iconography, no horned helmets, no fur-barbarian cliché, no crowns, no glowing magic effects, no pointed wizard hats, no modern clothing, no text besides the four name plaques, no photorealism, no anime style

## Hinweis

Diese vier stammen aus dem Regionalband. Die Prompts beschreiben sie bewusst über ihre Rolle und nicht als Nachbildung der offiziellen Illustrationen — gleich dir die Ergebnisse mit den Abbildungen in „Die Gestade des Gottwals" ab (S. 100–108) und passe Haarfarbe, Alter und Kleidung an, wenn dir etwas widerspricht.

Bei Ôle gilt dieselbe Vorsicht wie seinerzeit bei Schwester Minai: Bildmodelle greifen bei „Priester" sofort zur christlichen Ikonografie. Deshalb steht das Verbot dreifach im Prompt und in der Negativliste.
